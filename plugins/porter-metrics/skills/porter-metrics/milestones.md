## Milestones

The order matters: each one needs the one above it to exist. Someone can't
export a blend they never built, or schedule a report they never made.

| # | Milestone | What they get out of it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Integrations](integrations.md) | Their own data is in, from more than one place |
| 2 | [Reporting](reporting.md) | A live report at a URL they can send to someone |
| 3 | [Data Transformation](data-transformation.md) | Answers across accounts, not one platform at a time |
| 4 | [Data Export](data-export.md) | The data lands in Sheets or BigQuery on its own |
| 5 | [Automation](automation.md) | Porter does the work: research, changes, creative |
| 6 | [Notifications](notifications.md) | It arrives without anyone asking for it |
| 7 | [Daily routine](routine.md) | Porter starts their day, and closes the loop on it |

[Research](research.md) sits inside Automation but needs **no** data connected —
it works against public sources. It's the fastest way to show value to someone
who hasn't connected anything yet, and the right answer for a skeptic.

## Reaching one

A milestone is reached when the user has the **outcome**, not when the tool
returned 200. A report that exists but was never opened or shared isn't
Reporting; it's a row in a table. Show them the result — the numbers, the link —
and let them react to it before moving on.

Skip what [status](status.md) already shows done. Nothing costs more goodwill
than being walked through something you finished last week.

## Not in this list — yet

Four capabilities that belong in a complete activation story are absent because
Porter can't do them from the chat **today**: inviting a teammate, seeing your
account or credit limits, creating a calculated field, and alerts over Slack,
Teams or SMS. They are planned, not ruled out.

See [pending](pending.md) for what to say when a user asks for one — always with
the alternative that does work, and never with a date.
