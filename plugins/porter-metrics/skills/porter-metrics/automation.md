## 5 · Automation — Porter does the work

Everything past reading data. Three kinds, and they suit different people —
offer the one that matches what they told you, not all three.

| Kind | What it does | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| [Research](research.md) | SEO, ads and social intel on any brand, including competitors | Anyone. Needs **nothing connected** — the fastest way to show value |
| [Management](management.md) | Change campaigns, publish posts, update the CRM | People who currently do this by hand in five different tabs |
| [Generation](generation.md) | Images, video, voiceover | Anyone making creative, especially small teams without a designer |

All three live in the action catalog: `list_actions(task="…")` to find one,
`execute_action` to run it. The fixed tools are not the limit — search the
catalog before telling anyone Porter can't do something.

## What actually matters here

**Research first for a skeptic.** It needs no setup, no OAuth, no data. "Give me
a competitor's website and I'll tell you what they rank for" is a thirty-second
demo, and it's often what makes someone bother connecting their own accounts.

**Management is where trust is earned or lost.** These calls change real
campaigns and spend real money. Say exactly what you're about to change and get
a clear yes first — the name of the campaign, the old value and the new one.
Never batch several changes behind one confirmation.

**Read back what happened.** After a write, confirm the new state from Porter
rather than assuming the call did what you asked.
